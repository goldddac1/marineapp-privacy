This policy explains how MarineApp handles information when you use its community, messaging, vessel and CV features.

Information we process

Account and profile information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your account identifier, name, email address and profile photo information. You may add your age, maritime specialty, nationality, education, certificates and visas. These additional profile details are optional.

Community and messaging information. We store your crew/friend connections, conversation information, messages, read status, vessel comments and feedback you send us. Messages sent by the current Android app are encrypted before upload. Messages from older app versions may use an older storage format.

CV information. If you use the CV feature, we process the information you enter or import from your profile, including your name, position, contact email, phone number, city and country, availability, professional summary, sea service, education, certificates, languages and visas. CV use is optional. We save your CV draft and generate and store the PDF on our server.

Vessel preferences and service activity. We process saved vessels, arrival/departure preferences, search requests and basic request activity to provide these features and enforce service limits. Vessel positions shown on the map are vessel data. The current Android app does not request access to your device's GPS location.

Notifications and technical information. If you enable notifications, we process an app-installation identifier, notification token, account binding, app version and notification preferences. Our infrastructure and service providers also process technical information such as IP addresses, app and SDK versions, request information and security-related records to operate, maintain and protect the service.

Optional Google Drive recovery backup. If you connect Google Drive, MarineApp creates and reads a recovery file in its app-specific folder in your Google Drive account. The file contains account identifiers and the key material needed to restore access to encrypted chats. This recovery backup relies on the security of your Google account; it does not have separate end-to-end protection from the storage provider or a separate backup password. Disconnecting Drive does not necessarily delete an existing recovery file.

How information is used

We use information to sign you in and manage your account; display community profiles; find people and connect conversations; deliver messages and notifications; show vessel information; save your preferences; generate your CV; restore chat access when you request it; respond to feedback and support requests; and prevent abuse or unauthorized access.

We do not sell your personal information or use it to target advertising. The current Android app does not include Firebase Analytics for tracking screens or search behavior. This does not prevent necessary technical processing by our infrastructure and service providers.

Who can access information

Community profiles and comments are accessible to other signed-in MarineApp users. In the current profile storage, this includes the profile email address and connection identifiers, even when an app screen does not display those fields. Treat information in your community profile as community-visible.

Message records are restricted to the conversation participants and the systems needed to provide messaging. Optional recovery backups contain the material needed to recover message access, so you should protect access to your Google account.

Information entered only in your private CV draft is not added to your community profile. Access through the CV service is restricted to your authenticated account. Our hosting systems and authorized service administration process the draft and PDF to provide the feature. When you use Share or save a PDF to another service, the recipients or services you choose receive that copy under their own practices.

We use Google Sign-In, Firebase and Firebase Cloud Messaging for authentication, storage and notifications; Google Drive and Google Forms for optional recovery backups and deletion requests; and hosting, network and map infrastructure providers to deliver the service. Map requests use OpenFreeMap. These providers receive information needed to process the relevant requests. Google and other providers handle their services under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to address fraud, abuse or threats to the service or its users.

Storage and security

The current app uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections when transferring information to its services. We use authentication and access controls for private account features. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

We retain account information and associated content while needed to provide the service. CV drafts and the current generated PDF remain on our server until replaced or deleted as part of account deletion. Service and security records may be retained as needed to enforce limits, prevent abuse and investigate problems. Providers may retain their own records under their applicable policies.

Copies of PDFs, messages or other content that you or another person save outside MarineApp are controlled by the person or service holding those copies.

Your choices and account deletion

You can update your profile, choose whether to add optional details, choose whether to create a CV, manage saved vessels and notification preferences, and choose whether to connect Google Drive. You can manage notification permission in Android settings. You can remove MarineApp's stored recovery data through Google Drive's app-management settings.

To request deletion of your MarineApp account and associated data, open Settings > Delete account in the app or use:
https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSf-FuVAs-W_os9NdpxE94jok7azhRAd7WMx4TpA76rLZJVURw/viewform

You can also contact golddacetup@gmail.com from the email address used for the account. We verify account ownership before deletion; never send us your password or sign-in codes. Requests are handled manually, with deletion completed within 30 days after ownership is confirmed.

Deletion covers the account, profile, associated conversations and comments held in our service, uploaded avatar, private CV draft and PDF, saved vessel preferences and notification registrations. Minimal records needed to enforce an account deletion or protect the service may remain for those purposes. Deletion-request correspondence and completion records are retained for up to 90 days after completion, unless a legal requirement requires longer retention.

Recovery files in your own Google Drive and copies already saved or shared outside MarineApp may need to be deleted separately. Uninstalling MarineApp does not submit an account-deletion request.

For questions, access or correction requests, contact golddacetup@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable data-protection law.
